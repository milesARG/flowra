Flowra 1.0.0 - local voice dictation for Windows

Flowra runs speech recognition entirely on your computer. Your audio and text are
never sent anywhere. On first launch it downloads an open speech model from
Hugging Face (Systran/faster-whisper), after which it works offline.

Your settings and dictation history are stored in %APPDATA%\Flowra.
